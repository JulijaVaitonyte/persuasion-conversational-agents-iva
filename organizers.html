Meet the organizers

The workshop is organized by researchers working across human–agent interaction, machine learning, behavioural science, and management research.

Dr Julija Vaitonytė

Tilburg University, The Netherlands

Julija Vaitonytė is a Lecturer in the Department of Cognitive Science and Artificial Intelligence at Tilburg University and is also affiliated with ISM University of Management and Economics. Her research examines virtual agents, human–agent interaction, trust, and the social effects of conversational agents using behavioural and neuroscientific experiments and computational modelling.

J.Vaitonyte@tilburguniversity.edu

Dr Judita Rudokaitė

Amsterdam UMC, The Netherlands

Judita Rudokaitė is a Postdoctoral Researcher at Amsterdam UMC, where she develops offline reinforcement-learning models for high-stakes clinical decision support. She is also a co-founder of AINAR, a smartphone game designed to help people overcome needle fear using biofeedback methods.

j.rudokaite@amsterdamumc.nl

Prof. dr. Vita Akstinaitė

ISM University of Management and Economics, Lithuania

Vita Akstinaitė applies machine learning and big-data analytics to social-science research. Her work includes leadership expression and the effects of AI and social robots on people, including collaborative human–agent environments. She received a Fulbright Scholar award in 2023 and a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Fellowship in 2024.

vita.akstinaite@ism.lt
