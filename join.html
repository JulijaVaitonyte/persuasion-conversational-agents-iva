How participation works

This is a collaborative forum rather than a traditional paper workshop. Anyone interested in persuasion and social influence in Conversational Agents is welcome to register, subject to IVA 2026 attendance and registration arrangements.

During registration, you will be invited to share a short reflection of approximately 100–250 words. It may describe a research question, conceptual difficulty, an example from your work, a methodological need, or another issue that you believe the workshop should discuss.

These reflections are not formal submissions. They will not be peer reviewed, archived, or treated as conference publications. Their purpose is to help participants arrive with ideas already in mind and to help the organizers refine discussion themes before the workshop.

How to register

Registration to the workshop is handled via Eventbrite, which helps us estimate attendance and collect discussion topics before the workshop. Registering via Eventbrite does not constitute registration for IVA 2026 and does not by itself guarantee participation.

All workshop participants must also complete the official IVA 2026 conference registration.

What could you contribute?

Your reflection can be exploratory. Work in progress, unresolved questions, and cross-disciplinary perspectives are especially welcome.

  • A persuasive interaction pattern that current studies or benchmarks fail to capture.
  • A question about where persuasion ends and manipulation, deception, or a conversational dark pattern begins.
  • A challenge in studying behavioural or psychological effects over repeated or long-term interaction.
  • A subjective, behavioural, computational, or physiological measure that could improve evaluation.
  • A concrete case from healthcare, education, companionship, marketing, decision support, or another application area.

Optional reflection

What question, challenge, example, or methodological idea should the workshop address when developing a research agenda for persuasion in conversational agents?

If you choose to contribute a reflection, we ask that it be approximately 100–250 words.

How your input will be used

Before the workshop, the organizers will group the submitted reflections into broad themes and use them to refine discussion prompts and the roundtable structure.

Recurring themes may be summarized during the workshop, but individual contributions will not be publicly attributed or reproduced without permission.

Important: Participating in the reflection activity does not constitute a paper submission or publication. It is entirely optional, and participants are welcome to attend the workshop even if they do not submit a reflection in advance.

Who should participate?

We welcome researchers at all career stages working in Socially Interactive Agents, Conversational AI, Human–Computer Interaction, AI Safety, Cognitive Psychology, Neuroscience, Sociology, Marketing, and related fields.

You do not need to identify primarily as a persuasion researcher. Research on trust, social influence, companionship, decision-making, behaviour change, agent evaluation, and human flourishing is highly relevant.
